# Asset Sources & Licenses

Every third-party asset shipped with Kings of the Keep is listed here and in
[`public/assets/asset_manifest.json`](../public/assets/asset_manifest.json)
(the manifest is the machine-readable source of truth; this file is the
human-readable license record).

## License policy

- **Preferred:** CC0 / public domain.
- **Allowed:** CC-BY 3.0 / 4.0 — attribution recorded below and in the manifest.
- **Never:** NC, ND, CC-BY-SA, GPL-only art, unclear licenses, ripped or
  fan-made models of existing games/IP (Warcraft, Age of Empires, Total War, etc.).
- Raw downloads are kept in `assets_raw/`; runtime copies (GLB / 1K JPG) live
  under `public/assets/`. Models load by manifest ID via
  `src/render/assets/ModelLibrary.ts` — never by hardcoded path.
